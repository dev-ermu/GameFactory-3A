# GameA Fighting Arena — General Requirement

## Game Overview
- **Game ID**: gameA_fighting_arena_unity
- **Genre**: 3D side-scrolling versus fighting game (双人对战格斗)
- **Visual Reference**: Mortal Kombat (真人快打) — realistic, gritty style
- **Engine**: Unity3D 2022.3 URP (Universal Render Pipeline)
- **Players**: P1 vs P2 (local) or P1 vs AI

## Core Gameplay
- Side-view camera with limited depth movement (forward/backward within ~5 meters)
- Compact KOF-style 2.5D movement and combat rhythm without copying proprietary
  characters, art, stages, or exact move data
- Multi-level arena platforms that both fighters can jump onto and fight from
- Double jump, ground dash, and one air dash per airtime
- Distinct light/heavy punches and light/heavy kicks with different startup,
  reach, damage, and recovery
- Fighters have 200 HP; light attacks deal 3 damage, heavy attacks deal 5,
  and ultimates deal 20
- Each continuous guard fully blocks three hits; the fourth breaks through
  until the player releases and presses block again
- Normal attacks charge power by actual unblocked damage; 60 power enables
  the ultimate
- Best-of-5 rounds; first to win 3 rounds wins the match
- Each round has a 99-second countdown timer
- When HP reaches 0, the round ends with a K.O.
- If timer expires, the fighter with more HP wins the round

## Characters

### Character A — Muscular Warrior (肌肉武士)
- Muscular male warrior in traditional Japanese fighting hakama (袴)
- Bare-chested with arm bandages and battle tattoos
- Aggressive expression, short hair
- Fighting style: powerful, heavy-hitting strikes
- Ultimate skill: Fire Punch (火焰拳) — fist engulfs in flame, deals massive damage

### Character B — Ninja (忍者)
- Lean male ninja in full black bodysuit
- Face mask covering everything except the eyes
- Ninja headband, wrist gauntlets with hidden blades
- Short sword carried on the back
- Fighting style: agile, fast strikes
- Ultimate skill: Blade Draw Slash (拔刀斩) — draws blade with cyan energy arc

## Motion Set (7 actions per character, 14 total)
1. idle - stationary chest-level fighting guard
2. move - the same guard with a simple alternating footstep cycle
3. light_punch - one compact punch with small body rotation
4. heavy_punch - one broad punch with larger body rotation
5. kick - one direct front kick from the guard
6. block - stationary two-forearm chest guard
7. ultimate - one character-specific unarmed charged strike

Motion prompts describe body movement only. They do not mention weapons,
props, VFX, sound, cameras, or scene content. Idle, move, and block are loops;
the other motions are one-shots that return to the same base guard. Unity owns
gameplay locomotion, so all generated clips are in-place at 云端动作生成's native
20 FPS.

## Scene
- Japanese stone fighting arena (格斗擂台)
- Compact main stone ring plus two raised jumpable side platforms and a rear
  platform within the limited-depth combat lane
- All playable surfaces have reliable colliders and visible height changes
- Decorative architecture is grouped tightly around the arena without blocking
  the main fighting lane
- All three visible rear buildings are open, enterable secondary combat spaces
  connected to the main ring; no visible house is a sealed facade
- The rear architecture includes a realistic two-story, open-front fighting
  pavilion with solid side/rear walls, a usable staircase, and a continuous
  upper platform reachable by either stairs or the fighter's double jump
- Generated pavilion geometry owns presentation only; simple engine-authored
  collision volumes guarantee standable floors, climbable steps, solid walls,
  and an unobstructed open front
- Background: ancient Japanese architecture (torii gates, stone lanterns, bamboo grove)
- Red structural columns and all visible architecture are solid obstacles
- Stone lanterns are reusable combat props: solid while idle, pickable with
  two hands, throwable for damage, and pickable again after landing
- Stone slab floor with crack details
- Even ambient lighting, minimal shadows (characters always clearly visible)
- Warm color tones, center arena marker
- Limited depth (~5 meters forward/backward movement range)

## VFX (Visual Effects)
- Punch impact: shockwave particles (white circular spread)
- Kick impact: arc energy trail
- Block: blue hexagonal shield particles
- Warrior ultimate — Fire Punch:
  - Fist flame particles (orange-red, growing)
  - Flame trail on punch swing
  - Flame burst on impact
- Ninja ultimate — Blade Draw Slash:
  - Blade trail (cyan-white arc light blade)
  - Purple rune particles during hand seals
  - Air crack effect on impact

## Game Mechanics (C# Unity Assembly)
- FightingCharacterController — character control (movement, attack, block)
- FightingInputHandler — P1 keyboard / P2 keyboard or AI
- FightingCombatSystem — hit detection, damage calculation, hitstun frames
- FightingRoundManager — round management (timer, win/loss, best-of-5)
- FightingCameraController — side-view camera following midpoint of both fighters
- FightingComboSystem — combo system (light punch → heavy punch → flying kick)
- FightingUltimateSkill — ultimate skill system (charge then release, triggers CG)
- FightingAIBehavior — AI opponent behavior tree (attack, defend, move decisions)

## UI Design
- HP bars: top-left and top-right, with character portraits, flash white on hit
- Ultimate energy bar: below HP bar, highlights when full
- Round indicator and score: center top, current round plus P1/P2 round wins
- Timer: large center-top countdown, separated from the score
- Character name plates: above HP bars
- Ultimate cinematic: full-screen overlay + character close-up + subtitle

## MVP Playable Loop
Launch → P1/P2 Spawn → Fight → HP → 0 → Round End → Best of 5 → Match End → Restart

## Acceptance Criteria
- Unity launches without compilation errors
- Two characters spawn at start positions
- P1/P2 can move with A/D and arrow keys
- W/S controls depth movement
- Fighters can land on raised platforms, fall when stepping off, and cannot
  walk through platform sides
- First and second jump work; ground dash and one air dash work
- Light/heavy punches and light/heavy kicks deal distinct damage
- Red columns and idle stone lanterns block fighter movement
- Fighters can pick up, carry, and throw stone lanterns; a hit deals damage
  and hit stun, and the lantern becomes reusable after landing
- A continuous block negates three hits and the fourth hit breaks through
- Hit reaction animations play on hit
- Knockdown triggered at low HP
- Timer counts down from 99
- K.O. ends round when HP reaches 0
- Best of 5, first to 3 round wins works correctly
- Energy bar fills from actual unblocked normal-attack damage
- Ultimate available at 60 energy with VFX
- Top-left/right character portraits, HP bars, ultimate charge bars, timer, and
  round indicator are always visible and update in real time
- Press Enter on match end to restart
