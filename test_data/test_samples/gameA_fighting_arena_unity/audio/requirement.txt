# Audio Generation Requirements — gameA_fighting_arena_unity

## Overview
Generate character dialogue (TTS) and game sound effects (SFX) for the fighting
arena game. Dialogue uses Seed Audio; sound effects use Seed Audio.

## Model Backend
- **Dialogue**: Seed Audio (text → speech)
- **Sound Effects**: Seed Audio (text prompt → sound effect)

## Audio Assets

### Character Dialogue (TTS)
Both characters have ultimate skill voice lines and combat vocalizations.
All dialogue is in Chinese (中文).

#### Warrior (Speaker: muscular, deep, rough male voice)
- Ultimate line: "被我的拳头放倒吧！" (aggressive, powerful)
- Battle cry: "哈！" (short exertion, punching)
- Hit grunt: "呃！" (pain, being hit)
- Victory: "这就是力量的差距！" (triumphant)
- Defeat: "不可能……" (weak, disbelieving)

#### Ninja (Speaker: cold, low, menacing male voice)
- Ultimate line: "接受你的命运吧" (cold, threatening)
- Battle cry: "喝！" (short, sharp)
- Hit grunt: "唔！" (muffled pain)
- Victory: "弱者没有资格站起来。" (dismissive)
- Defeat: "下次……不会……" (fading)

### Sound Effects (SFX)

#### Combat Hits
- Punch impact: sharp shockwave, heavy thud
- Kick impact: arc whoosh + impact
- Block: metallic deflect ping
- Knockdown: heavy body fall

#### Ultimate Skills
- Fire punch charge: flame ignition, crackling fire
- Fire punch impact: flame burst, explosion
- Blade draw: metallic unsheath + energy hum
- Blade slash: energy slash whoosh + air crack

#### UI / System
- Round start bell: traditional Japanese bell
- FIGHT! announcement: deep impact sound
- K.O. announcement: dramatic hit
- Round end: bell
- Timer tick (last 10 seconds): urgent ticking

#### Environment
- Arena ambient: wind, distant bamboo rustle, stone ambiance

## Output Format
- Dialogue: WAV, 24000 Hz sample rate
- Sound effects: WAV, 48000 Hz sample rate
- Duration: 1-5 seconds per SFX, 2-5 seconds per dialogue

## Output Path
- test_data/outputs/gameA_fighting_arena_unity/<run_id>/assets/audio/<task_id>/audio.wav
