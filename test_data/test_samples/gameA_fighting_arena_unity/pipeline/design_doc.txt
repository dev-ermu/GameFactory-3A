# GameA Fighting Arena — Game Design Brief

## Page 1: Concept & Gameplay

### Game Vision
GameA Fighting Arena is a 3D side-scrolling versus fighting game inspired by
Mortal Kombat, built on Unity3D 2022.3 URP. Two fighters — a muscular warrior
and a lean ninja — face off in a traditional Japanese stone arena. The game
delivers visceral, weighty combat with combo chains, ultimate skills, and
dramatic cinematic moments.

### Core Gameplay Loop
Launch → P1/P2 Spawn → Fight (move, attack, block, combo) → HP reaches 0 →
Round End → Best of 5 → Match End → Restart

Each match is best-of-5 rounds. Each round has a 99-second timer. When a
fighter's HP reaches 0, the round ends with a K.O. If the timer expires, the
fighter with more HP wins the round. The first fighter to win 3 rounds wins
the match.

### Camera & Perspective
Side-view camera (横版) positioned perpendicular to the fighting plane. The
camera follows the midpoint between both fighters and zooms based on their
distance. Characters can move horizontally (left/right) and in limited depth
(forward/backward within ~5 meters), always facing each other.

The arena uses a compact multi-level 2.5D layout. Fighters can jump onto raised
platforms, double jump, ground dash, and air dash once before landing. Light and
heavy punches and kicks create distinct close-range options in a KOF-style
combat rhythm while retaining original characters, stage, balance values, and
visual assets.

### Characters

**Character A — Muscular Warrior (肌肉武士)**
A bare-chested, heavily muscled warrior wearing a traditional Japanese fighting
hakama (袴). Arm bandages wrap his forearms; battle tattoos cover his torso.
His fighting style is powerful and deliberate — heavy punches, devastating
kicks, and slow but impactful movements. His ultimate skill, the Fire Punch
(火焰拳), engulfs his fist in flame for a massive damage strike.

**Character B — Ninja (忍者)**
A lean, agile fighter in a full black bodysuit with a face mask revealing only
the eyes. A ninja headband sits on his forehead, and wrist gauntlets conceal
hidden blades. A short sword rests on his back. His fighting style is fast and
fluid — quick strikes, rapid movement, and precise techniques. His ultimate
skill, the Blade Draw Slash (拔刀斩), unleashes a cyan energy arc that rends the
air itself.

### Combat System
- **Health**: Each fighter starts every round with 200 HP
- **Light Punch** (J/Numpad1): Quick, low-damage strike (3 damage)
- **Heavy Punch** (K/Numpad2): Slower, higher-damage hook (5 damage)
- **Light Kick** (L/Numpad3): Medium-range quick kick (3 damage)
- **Heavy Kick** (O/Numpad6): Committed kick (5 damage)
- **Jump / Double Jump** (Space/Numpad0): Up to two jumps before landing
- **Dash** (Left Shift/Numpad Enter): Ground dash or one air dash per airtime
- **Block** (U/Numpad4): Each press fully negates up to three hits; the fourth
  hit breaks through until block is released and pressed again
- **Combo System**: Light Punch → Heavy Punch → Flying Kick chains for bonus damage
- **Ultimate** (I/Numpad5): Available at 60 energy, deals 20 damage with VFX
- **Pick Up Prop** (E/Numpad7): Lift the nearest reusable stone lantern
- **Throw Prop** (F/Numpad8): Throw the carried lantern for 18 damage and hit stun
- **Hit Reactions**: Directional hit stun, knockdown at low HP
- **Energy**: Normal attacks add the actual unblocked damage dealt; blocked
  attacks add 0 and an ultimate does not refund energy into itself

### Scene
A compact multi-level stone fighting platform surrounded by railings, set in an
ancient Japanese courtyard. Two raised side platforms and a shallow rear ledge
are real playable surfaces. Torii gates, stone lanterns, and a bamboo grove form
the tightly composed background. Three open rear dojo bays are connected
secondary combat spaces rather than sealed facades. The floor is cracked stone slab with a center marking. Lighting is
even and ambient with minimal shadows — fighters must always be clearly
visible. Warm color tones pervade the arena.

All red structural columns are solid. The two stone lanterns are reusable
combat props: they block movement while idle, can be lifted and carried by the
gameplay system without dedicated generated character motions, deal damage
when thrown, and become pickable again after landing on any playable surface.

## Page 2: Technical & Production

### Engine & Architecture
- **Engine**: Unity3D 2022.3 LTS, Universal Render Pipeline (URP)
- **Code Assembly**: FightingArenaGameplay.dll (C#)
- **UI Module**: FightingArenaUI (engine-native + Browser Play)
- **Asset Pipeline**: AI-generated assets imported into Unity prefabs

### AI Asset Generation Pipeline
1. **T-pose Images**: QwenEdit + RMBG → clean T-pose reference images (RGBA PNG)
2. **3D Objects**: Tripo → character/weapon/prop GLB models (≤100k tris, PBR 2048)
3. **Motions**: MoMask -> BVH -> Puppeteer rig + bpy retarget -> animation FBX (7 core body motions per character; 14 total)
4. **Scene**: Hunyuan-WorldPlay2 → stone arena scene GLB
5. **Audio**: Qwen3-TTS (dialogue) + Woosh-DFlow (SFX) → WAV files
6. **CG Video**: Seedance → ultimate skill showcase video (CG-4)

### Game Mechanics (9 C# Classes)
1. FightingCharacterController — movement, attack, block, state machine
2. FightingInputHandler — P1/P2 keyboard input routing + AI toggle
3. FightingCombatSystem — hitbox detection, damage, hitstun, knockdown
4. FightingRoundManager — timer, K.O., best-of-5, match flow
5. FightingCameraController — side-view midpoint tracking camera
6. FightingComboSystem — timed combo chains with damage scaling
7. FightingUltimateSkill — energy charge, CG trigger, ultimate damage
8. FightingAIBehavior — behavior tree for AI opponent (approach/attack/defend)
9. FightingThrowableProp — reusable stone-lantern carry, throw, impact, and landing

### UI Design
- HP bars (top-left/right) with portraits, flash white on damage
- Energy bars below HP, segmented, glow when full
- Round indicator and score (center top): current round, P1/P2 wins, "FIGHT!", "K.O."
- Timer (center): 99→0 countdown, red pulse at ≤10s
- Ultimate cinematic: full-screen overlay, character close-up, subtitle
- Match end: winner portrait, "Press ENTER to Restart"

### VFX
- Punch impact: white shockwave particle spread
- Kick impact: arc energy trail
- Block: blue hexagonal shield particles
- Fire Punch: flame ignition → flame trail → flame burst on impact
- Blade Draw Slash: cyan-white arc + purple rune particles + air crack

### Acceptance Criteria (28 checks)
Launch (3), Movement (4), Combat (5), Round (5), Ultimate (4), UI (4), Restart (3)

### MVP Playable Loop
Launch → P1/P2 Spawn → Fight → HP → 0 → Round End → Best of 5 → Match End → Restart
Every node must have a verifiable pass condition. Pipeline reports failure and
triggers Repair if any node breaks.
