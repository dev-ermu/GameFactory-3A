# Motion Requirements - gameD_racing_unity

## Status: NOT APPLICABLE FOR THE MVP

`motion_tasks.jsonl` is intentionally empty. Vehicle and driver motion is
procedural Unity behavior generated with the mechanic package:

- wheel spin derives from vehicle speed when independent wheel nodes exist;
- front-wheel and steering-wheel rotation derive from steering input;
- body roll derives from lateral acceleration;
- the driver uses a seated pose and hand IK targets;
- falling or flipped cars reset to the latest safe checkpoint.

No 云端动作生成, BVH, retargeted FBX, or authored AnimationClip is required.
