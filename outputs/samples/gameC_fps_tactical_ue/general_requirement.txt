# gameC_fps_tactical_ue — General Requirement

## Game Overview
- **Game ID**: gameC_fps_tactical_ue
- **Genre**: First-person tactical shooter (第一人称射击)
- **Visual Reference**: Call of Duty (使命召唤) — realistic military style
- **Engine**: Unreal Engine 5
- **Player**: Single player vs AI enemies

## Core Gameplay
- First-person perspective with mouse look
- Player is a counter-terrorism operative in tactical gear
- Enemies are terrorists dressed in all black
- Weapon: generated shotgun (霰弹枪)
- Weapon hold: one hand on the pistol grip, one hand on the foregrip/stock area
- Clear all enemies in the area to win
- Movement, shooting, reload mechanics
- Win condition: eliminate all enemies
- Lose condition: player HP reaches 0

## Characters

### Player — Counter-Terrorism Operative (反恐特战)
- Wears counter-terrorism tactical vest and gear
- First-person view: only hands and weapon visible
- Hands positioned: one on pistol grip (rear), one on foregrip/stock area (front)

### Enemy — Terrorist (恐怖分子)
- Dressed in all black clothing
- Holds rifle, patrols the area
- Has idle, walk, shoot, hit reaction, and death animations
- Death animation: falls forward or backward

## Weapon
- **Shotgun (霰弹枪)**: Generated 3D model
- Pump-action shotgun with tactical black finish
- High detail, PBR textures
- One hand on pistol grip, one hand on foregrip/stock position
- Fire: spread pellet shots, high close-range damage
- Reload: pump action animation

## Motion Set
### Enemy Motions (5 actions)
1. idle - standing with rifle, alert stance
2. walk - walking with rifle shouldered
3. shoot - firing rifle
4. hit_react - stagger from bullet impact
5. death - falls forward/backward

### Player Hand Motions (3 actions)
1. shotgun_idle - hands holding shotgun in ready position
2. shotgun_shoot - fire shotgun, recoil
3. shotgun_reload - pump action reload

## Scene
- Imported open-source abandoned prison map
- Indoor corridors, cell blocks, open common areas
- Dark, atmospheric lighting with contrast shadows
- Destructible environment props (barrels, crates)
- Doors that can be opened
- Realistic concrete and metal materials

## VFX (Visual Effects)
- Muzzle flash: fire particle on each shot
- Bullet impact: spark particles on walls, blood on enemies
- Shotgun pellet spread: multiple impact points
- Damage vignette: red screen edge when player takes damage

## Game Mechanics (UE5 Blueprint / C++)
- FPSPlayerController — first-person movement (walk, run, jump, crouch)
- WeaponSystem — shotgun firing, reload, recoil, spread
- FPSHitSystem — hit detection, headshot multiplier, damage zones
- EnemyAI — patrol, detect player, shoot, seek cover
- EnemySpawner — enemy spawn management
- FPSDamageSystem — player health, damage vignette, death
- FPSHUDController — ammo, health, objective display

## UI Design
- Crosshair: center screen, shotgun spread indicator
- Ammo: bottom-right, "shells remaining / reserve"
- Health: bottom-left or screen-edge red vignette
- Objective: top-left "Eliminate all enemies"
- Kill notification: center "KILL +100"
- Game over screen: "Mission Complete" / "You Died" + restart prompt

## Controls

| Action | Key |
|--------|-----|
| Move | WASD |
| Run | Shift |
| Jump | Space |
| Crouch | Ctrl |
| Shoot | Left Mouse |
| Aim | Right Mouse |
| Reload | R |
| Interact (doors) | E |
| Pause | Esc |

## MVP Playable Loop
Launch → Spawn → Find Enemies → Combat → Clear Area → Mission Complete → Restart

## Acceptance Criteria
- UE5 launches without compilation errors
- Player spawns with shotgun visible in first person
- WASD moves player, mouse controls view
- Left click fires shotgun with muzzle flash
- Shotgun has spread pattern (multiple pellets)
- R reloads shotgun
- Enemies spawn in the scene
- Enemies patrol when player not detected
- Enemies shoot at player when detected
- Shooting enemy causes damage and hit reaction
- Headshot causes extra damage
- Enemy death animation plays
- All enemies killed → mission complete
- Player takes damage from enemy fire
- Player death → game over screen
- Crosshair, ammo count, health indicator visible
- Kill notification appears
- Restart works from game over screen
