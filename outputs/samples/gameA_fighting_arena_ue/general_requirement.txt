# gameA_fighting_arena_ue — General Requirement

## Game Overview
- **Game ID**: gameA_fighting_arena_ue
- **Genre**: 3D one-on-one boxing/fighting game (双人格斗)
- **Visual Reference**: Mortal Kombat (真人快打) — realistic combat style
- **Engine**: Unreal Engine 5
- **Players**: P1 vs P2 (local) or P1 vs AI

## Core Gameplay
- Side-view camera with 3D scene movement
- Two fighters face off in an open-air medieval arena
- Normal attacks are regular punches (普通挥拳)
- Landing hits charges the ultimate energy meter
- When energy is full, the ultimate can be activated: a fire-charged uppercut (火焰上勾拳)
- Both characters share the same ultimate mechanic: flaming fist uppercut with fire VFX
- Best-of-3 rounds; first to win 2 rounds wins the match
- Each round has a 99-second countdown timer
- When HP reaches 0, the round ends with a K.O.
- If timer expires, the fighter with more HP wins the round

## Characters

### Character A — Warrior (武士)
- Muscular male warrior, bare-chested with arm bandages
- Aggressive expression, short hair
- Fighting style: powerful, heavy-hitting punches
- Idle stance: combat guard with fists raised
- Ultimate skill: Fire Punch Uppercut (火焰上勾拳) — fist engulfs in flame, delivers an upward strike

### Character B — Armored Goddess (铠甲女神)
- Female fighter wearing ornate armor (铠甲)
- Confident expression, flowing hair
- Fighting style: agile, precise strikes
- Idle stance: combat guard with fists raised
- Ultimate skill: Fire Punch Uppercut (火焰上勾拳) — same fire effect, flaming uppercut

## Motion Set (5 actions per character, 10 total)
1. idle - combat guard stance, fists raised, slight breathing movement
2. move - normal running toward/away from opponent
3. punch - regular forward punch (普通挥拳)
4. ultimate - fire-charged uppercut (火焰上勾拳)
5. hit_react - stagger back from impact

All motions are in-place at generation FPS. UE5 owns gameplay locomotion and
root motion.

## Scene
- Imported open-source medieval exterior map (Sicka Yazd environment)
- Open-air stone arena with medieval architecture
- Stone floor with structural columns
- Ambient lighting keeps both fighters clearly visible
- Warm color tones, arena center marker
- Limited depth movement range (~5 meters forward/backward)

## VFX (Visual Effects)
- Punch impact: small impact particle burst
- Ultimate — Fire Punch Uppercut (both characters):
  - Fist engulfs in flame particles (orange-red, growing)
  - Flame trail on the uppercut swing
  - Flame burst explosion on impact
- Hit reaction: small spark particles on contact

## Game Mechanics (UE5 Blueprint / C++)
- FightCharacterController — character control (movement, attack, block)
- FightInputHandler — P1 keyboard / P2 keyboard or AI
- FightCombatSystem — hit detection, damage calculation, hitstun
- FightRoundManager — round management (timer, win/loss, best-of-3)
- FightCameraController — side-view camera following midpoint of both fighters
- FightUltimateSkill — ultimate skill system (charge then release, fire VFX)
- FightAIBehavior — AI opponent behavior (approach, attack, retreat decisions)

## UI Design
- HP bars: top-left and top-right, flash white on hit
- Ultimate energy bar: below HP bar, highlights when full
- Round indicator: center top, current round and P1/P2 round wins
- Timer: center countdown from 99
- Character name plates: above HP bars
- Ultimate cinematic: brief screen effect when ultimate activates
- Match end screen: winner announcement, "Press Enter to Restart"

## Controls

| Action | P1 | P2 |
|--------|----|----|
| Move | A / D | ← / → |
| Move Depth | W / S | ↑ / ↓ |
| Punch | J | Numpad 1 |
| Block | U | Numpad 4 |
| Ultimate | I (when energy full) | Numpad 5 |
| Pause | Esc | — |

## MVP Playable Loop
Launch → P1/P2 Spawn → Fight → HP → 0 → Round End → Best of 3 → Match End → Restart

## Acceptance Criteria
- UE5 launches without compilation errors
- Two characters spawn at start positions
- P1/P2 can move with A/D and arrow keys
- Characters face each other
- Punch deals damage to opponent
- Hit reaction plays on hit
- Energy bar fills from landing punches
- Ultimate available at full energy with fire VFX
- Timer counts down from 99
- K.O. ends round when HP reaches 0
- Best of 3, first to 2 wins works correctly
- HP bars update in real time
- Round indicator updates
- FIGHT text appears at round start
- Press Enter on match end to restart
